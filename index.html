About Nereus LLC

Building Bridges in Global Trade

Based in the United States, Nereus LLC is a commodity trading company specializing in plywood, aluminum, bronze, and copper. We connect producers and manufacturers across continents, ensuring consistent quality and competitive pricing.

With deep expertise in raw materials sourcing and international logistics, we serve as a dependable link in the global supply chain. Our commitment to transparency and reliability has earned us the trust of partners worldwide.

What We Trade

Our Core Products

We specialize in four key commodities — sourced globally and delivered with uncompromising standards of quality.

Plywood

Plywood

Premium-grade plywood sourced from trusted mills — available in birch, hardwood, and marine-grade varieties for construction and manufacturing.

Aluminum

Aluminum

High-quality aluminum sheets, coils, and extrusions for aerospace, automotive, and industrial applications. Multiple alloy grades available.

Bronze

Bronze

Durable bronze alloys in ingots, bars, and custom forms — ideal for marine hardware, bearings, and decorative architectural elements.

Copper

Copper

Pure copper cathodes, pipes, sheets, and wire rod. Sourced from certified producers for electrical, plumbing, and industrial use.
